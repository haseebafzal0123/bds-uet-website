About Us

Blood Donors' Society (BDS) is a welfare organization of UET Lahore, operated by students under the supervision of the university administration. Practically active since the 1980s, the first organizing body was formed in 1996, and BDS has been officially registered with UET since 2004.

We arrange voluntary blood donations across Lahore, maintaining a strong network of student volunteers and donors who collect, organize, and distribute blood to hospitals and individuals during emergencies — in close collaboration with blood banks and healthcare institutions.

What We Do

Blood Screening Camp

Annual event where students learn their blood group for free, alongside our induction drive for new members.

Newcomers' Orientation

Introducing new students to BDS, our work, and how they can become part of the society.

DFU — Disease Free UET

A 2-day medical camp where visiting doctors provide free checkups for students.

Ramzan Drive

Daily iftari arrangements, plus a Grand Iftar every Friday for 200+ people.
